Castle Siege PvP – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short
  • We show no ads, we don't track you across apps, and we don't sell your data.
  • We don't collect advertising IDs, location, contacts, photos, microphone data or email addresses.
  • Analytics and crash reports are sent only if you allow them; both start switched off and can be turned off at any time.
  • You can delete your account instantly in the game (Account deletion).

1. Data controller

The data controller operating the Castle Siege PvP game and its game server is Mahmut Turan (an individual developer).

Contact and data requests: omggamesstudio1@gmail.com. In the game, Settings › Privacy & Data › Request about my data opens a data request linked to your account; you see the answer in the game.

3. What we don't collect

  • Advertising IDs (IDFA/AAID) or persistent device identifiers
  • Location, contacts, photos, camera and microphone
  • Email address and real name (not even when you sign in with Apple or Google)
  • Card and payment details

There are no ads in the game. There is no tracking across apps and websites. Personal data is not sold and not used for marketing.

4. Consent: analytics and crash reports

You are asked on first launch. Both options start switched off; you can choose "Accept all", "Reject all", or pick them one by one and "Save my choice". No analytics data is sent or written to disk before you decide. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & Data; turning an option off deletes the data still waiting on your device. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.

5. Who we share data with

  • Apple and Google: Sign in with Apple, Google sign-in and in-app purchases are handled by these companies under their own privacy policies. Our server verifies sign-in tokens and Apple purchase signatures itself, using the public keys these companies publish, and sends them no personal data for that. Only to verify a Google Play purchase and learn about its refund, the purchase token Google issued for that purchase is sent to the Google Play Developer API.
  • Hosting provider: the game server runs at a hosting or cloud provider, which stores data only on our behalf (processor).
  • Authorities: only when required by law, and only as far as the law allows.

Other players can see your display name, trophies, league and battle power; your friends see whether you are online. Your online matches can be shown to other players in spectator mode and replays.

6. International transfers

When you choose to sign in with Apple or Google or to make a purchase, that action happens directly between you and that company. The only personal data our server sends abroad is the purchase token passed to Google when verifying a Google Play purchase; this is necessary to deliver what you bought (GDPR Art. 49(1)(b); KVKK Art. 9/6-b). If the game server is hosted outside your country, the transfer relies on the safeguards required by the GDPR and KVKK (such as standard contractual clauses), and this page will name the country where the server is located.

7. Security

  • The connection between the game and the server is encrypted (DTLS).
  • Session and guest keys are kept in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore); the server only stores hashes of them.
  • Only the data controller has access to server data; backups are kept for 14 days.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to receive a copy of your data in a portable format, and to withdraw consent.

  • In the game (immediate): turning off analytics and crash reports, "Delete my analytics data" and account deletion (Main menu › Account · Sign in › Delete account).
  • In the game (request): Settings › Privacy & Data › Request about my data. Your request is linked to your account, so no extra proof of identity is needed; the answer appears in the game.
  • By email: write to omggamesstudio1@gmail.com with your player ID. We may ask for extra information to confirm the account is yours.

Requests are free and answered without undue delay, within 30 days at the latest. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to your local data protection authority (in Türkiye: the Personal Data Protection Authority, kvkk.gov.tr).

9. Account and data deletion

Uninstalling the game does not delete your account on the server. The steps, and the records we keep, are on the Account deletion page.

10. Children

Castle Siege PvP is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you are below the age of digital consent in your country, give analytics consent together with a parent. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we delete it; parents can contact us.

11. This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and use no cookies. GitHub may keep technical access logs when serving the pages (GitHub Privacy Statement).

12. Changes

When we update this policy we change the date above and announce important changes in the game. We ask for your consent again for any new processing that needs it.
